Cabin Cast privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026. Developer: Hemal Modi. Contact: hemalmodi3@gmail.com.

Cabin Cast shows your iPhone screen in a car's built-in web browser over your iPhone's Personal Hotspot. This page says what the app does with your data. The short version: it collects none, and nothing leaves your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Cabin Cast has no account, no sign-in and no analytics. It has no advertising and no tracking. I do not run a server that the app talks to, so I never receive your screen, your hotspot details, your car or anything else.

Where the picture goes

When you tap Start Broadcast, iOS records your iPhone screen and hands the frames to Cabin Cast. The app sends them directly to the browser that opened its address on your hotspot, which is your car when the car has joined the hotspot. The frames are not saved, not stored and not sent anywhere else. Audio is never captured. When you stop the broadcast or lock your iPhone, the picture stops.

Anyone who knows your hotspot password and opens the address while a broadcast is running can see the picture. Keep your hotspot password private, and stop the broadcast when you are done.

The Car display link

Car browsers refuse to open the private address a hotspot gives your iPhone. To get around that, Cabin Cast adds one extra address to your iPhone using a system feature that iOS files under VPN. That is why iOS asks to "Add VPN Configurations" the first time you turn the link on. The link connects to no VPN service and no server. It carries no internet traffic, from your iPhone or from the car. It only lets the car reach the picture on your iPhone. You can remove it at any time in Settings, General, VPN & Device Management.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, such as the picture quality preset.
  • A small log of the last link and broadcast events, kept on the iPhone to help you troubleshoot. It is never sent anywhere.
  • A counter of finished sessions, used only to decide when to ask you for an App Store rating.
